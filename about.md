## Purpose of the archive

The Department of Bengali at Islamia Government College has taught the language
of <span lang="bn">বাংলা</span> — its literature, its grammar, its music and its
argument — for generations. Each batch passes through and then disperses, and
most of what made those years matter never gets written down anywhere.

This website is an attempt to change that for one batch. It is a digital
yearbook and a long-term archive: a place where memories, events, quotations,
photographs and small milestones can be kept in one readable, searchable
collection instead of scattering across group chats and personal phones.

## The batch's storytelling mission

- **Record what happened, honestly.** Entries describe real experiences. When a
  detail is uncertain, it is marked as approximate or left out.
- **Keep the language.** Memories and reflections may be written in Bengali,
  in English, or in both. Bengali content is published in Bengali, not flattened
  into translation.
- **Make it readable for everyone.** Every page is built to work on a low-cost
  phone, on a slow connection, with a keyboard or with a screen reader.
- **Respect the people in the story.** Consent is not a formality here; it is a
  publication requirement.

## What the archive may preserve

| Content type | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| Memories | First-person recollections, classroom moments, campus life, reflections |
| Events | Programmes, seminars, cultural evenings and gatherings, with dates and venues |
| Quotes | Public-domain literary quotations, and contributor reflections with permission |
| Photo gallery | Consent-cleared photographs with accessible descriptions |
| Our Journey | A chronological timeline of verified batch milestones |
| Batch Members | Optional, consent-based profiles of members |

## How members can contribute

Contributions are collected through a channel agreed by the batch, reviewed
before publication, and then committed to the public repository that builds
this site. The full process — what to prepare, what is checked, and what is
never published — is described on the Contribute a Memory page.

Because submissions are **not automated yet**, this website deliberately
contains no form that pretends to send data anywhere. Nothing you type on this
site is collected, stored or transmitted.

## How submissions are reviewed

1. **Accuracy** — names, dates, venues and quotations are checked against what
   the contributor supplied. Unsourced quotations are not published.
2. **Consent** — photographs are published only when everyone identifiable has
   agreed, and profiles only with the member's recorded consent.
3. **Privacy** — phone numbers, addresses, student identification numbers,
   examination results and anything that could expose a member to harm are
   removed or, where they are the point of the memory, the entry is not
   published at all.
4. **Tone and fairness** — entries that mock, blame or expose a named person
   without their agreement are not published.
5. **Rights** — text, images and translations must be the contributor's own
   work, in the public domain, or used with permission.

## Privacy and photo consent

Photographs of identifiable people are treated as personal data. Publication
requires permission, captions must be accurate, and anyone can ask for an image
or a memory to be corrected or removed. The full policy — including what can and
cannot be guaranteed once something has been published publicly — is on the
Privacy Policy page, with guidance for contributors in
`PRIVACY_AND_CONSENT.md` in the repository.

## Technical notes

- Built with Jekyll and deployed on GitHub Pages, so there is no server, no
  database and no paid hosting involved.
- No analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party tracking scripts.
- Fonts are self-hosted, so reading this site does not send a request to any
  third-party font service.
- The archive is a set of Markdown files with a documented schema, which keeps
  the content portable and readable even without this website.
