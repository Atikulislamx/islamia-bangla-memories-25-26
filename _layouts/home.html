About Us

Why this archive exists

The Department of Bengali at Islamia Government College has carried the language of বাংলা through generations of students. This archive keeps one batch's experience in that long line: how the classes felt, what was read, who was met, what was said.

It is a student project, maintained from a public repository, and it is not the official website of the college. Every contribution is reviewed for accuracy and consent before it is published.

Read the About page Search

What the archive keeps

  • Everyday memories, in English or Bengali
  • Events with dates, locations and photographs
  • Public-domain quotations and personal reflections
  • Consent-cleared photographs with accessible descriptions
  • A chronological timeline of verified milestones
