> **This is a structural placeholder, not a person.** The batch member profile

A real profile in this archive is short: the member's name (or a chosen form of
it), the course and batch, a few lines written by the member, and — only if they
want one — a photograph.

## How a real profile is published

1. The member writes the text themselves, or reviews what has been written
   about them.
2. The member confirms, in writing or through the batch's agreed channel, that
   the profile and photograph may appear on a public website.
3. Consent is recorded as a field in the profile file. A profile without
   recorded consent is not displayed anywhere on this website.
4. The member can change or withdraw consent later, and the profile is updated
   or removed.
