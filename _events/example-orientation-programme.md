The orientation introduces new students to the department: how honours classes
are organised, which teachers take which courses, how the seminar library works
and what the department expects from a first-year student.

## What the archive records from a programme

- The date and the venue, exactly as confirmed by the department or the batch.
- A short description of what the programme was for.
- Photographs, if everyone identifiable has agreed to appear.
- Links to memories written by members about the same occasion.

## What it does not record

Attendance lists, examination results, internal marks, personal contact details
or any private discussion of a student's performance. Where a photograph cannot
be published because consent is missing, the photograph is simply left out.
