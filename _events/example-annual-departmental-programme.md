The annual programme combines the academic and the cultural: results day
announcements, a short address by the department, recitations, a quiz and the
informal prize giving that everybody pretends not to care about.

## Recording a programme properly

For a real entry, the archive asks for the confirmed date, the venue, a
one-paragraph description, and a note of who organised it. Everything else —
photographs, memories, quotations read that day — is added as it arrives.

If a detail cannot be confirmed, it stays out of the entry rather than being
written from memory.
